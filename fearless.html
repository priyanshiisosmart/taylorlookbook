Hidden Details & Easter Eggs

  • The original Fearless liner notes contained hidden capital letters spelling out secret messages — a tradition Taylor has continued across every album since.
  • "Love Story" was written in 20 minutes in her bedroom after sneaking out to see a boy her parents didn't approve of. Romeo & Juliet — but with a happy ending.
  • The album title "Fearless" came from Taylor's belief that being fearless doesn't mean having no fears — it means jumping anyway.
  • The music video for "You Belong With Me" features Taylor playing both the nerdy girl and the popular girl — a nod to the duality she's always felt about herself.
  • Fearless (Taylor's Version) includes 6 "From the Vault" tracks — songs written during the era but left off the original. Each one sounds like it belongs.
  • The golden confetti that rained during the Fearless Tour was shaped like stars — the same stars on her guitar strap.
  • "Fifteen" was written for her best friend Abigail, who is still one of her closest friends. Abigail appears in the music video.
